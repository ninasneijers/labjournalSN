1 Exploratory Results

1.1 Model 0: Simple Model

The null model converges at 0.13, which is acceptable. The outcomes of the simple model (Table 1) show things we have already learned from the descriptive results and the network graph, namely that there is a significantly bigger chance that one does not observe a tie, compared to the chance that one would observe a tie, thus meaning that density is very low (b = -2.67, SE = 0.53, p >.01). The reciprocity statistic is positive, indicating that the students in the network do tend to reciprocate their tie (b = 2.09, SE = 0.14, p >.01)

Change in social satisfaction is not significant on a linear level (b = 0.17, SE = 0.14, p = 0.22), but it is significant on a quadratic level (b = -0.34, SE = 0.15, p = 0.02) and going into a negative direction, which indicates that students whose social satisfaction is below the mean have a stronger tendency to move towards to mean than students who are above the mean.

However, these statistics on their own do not mean a lot yet, as the statistics as defined in the methodology need to be added to the models and some additional control statistics.
